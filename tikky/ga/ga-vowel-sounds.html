Ga Wiemɔkuli — Vowel Sounds

Let's learn Ga words by listening to their special vowel sounds! 👂🌟

🔊 Pattern 1 — the ɔi sound
ɔi
Sounds like "oy" in "boy"!
👆 Tap a word to see it highlighted
bɔi gɔi hɔi kɔi
🌸
fɔfɔi
fɔ – fɔ – i
flower
🎃
abɔbɔi
a – bɔ – bɔ – i
pumpkin
🔊 Pattern 2 — the ɛi sound
ɛi
Sounds like "ay" in "day"!
Listen for the "ɛ" then "i" sound
fɛi dɛi ɔɛi jɛi sɛi
👅
lilɛi
li – lɛ – i
tongue
🐟
didɛi
di – dɛ – i
tilapia (fish)
🔊 Pattern 3 — the iɔ sound
iɔ
Sounds like "yo" — say "i" then "ɔ"!
Listen carefully — two vowels together
biɔ diɔ fiɔ jɔ
🩳
pioto
pi – o – to
shorts
🤫
dioo
di – o – o
silence
🐜
bibloo
bi – bi – o – o
small
