Ga — oi · ia · āi Patterns

Three new Ga vowel sounds and lots of new words! 👂🌟

🔊 Pattern 1 — the oi sound
oi
Sounds like "oy" — say o then i!
Listen carefully and repeat each word
loi foi joi koi
🐘
kooloi
ko – o – lo – i
animals
🦜
akoi
a – ko – i
parrots
🔊 Pattern 2 — the ia sound
ia
Say "i" then "a" — like "yah"!
Two vowels together make one smooth sound
bia dia fia hia
🐐
abotia
a – bo – ti – a
goat
📏
liamo
li – a – mo
line
🍲
adia
a – di – a
food
🔊 Pattern 3 — the āi sound
āi
Long "aa" then "i" — like "ah-ee"!
The ā is a long, held vowel sound
🍃
bai
ba – i
leaves
🧢
fai
fa – i
cap / rivers
🏹
gāi
gā – i
arrow
🙏
ofainɛ
o – fa – i – nɛ
please
