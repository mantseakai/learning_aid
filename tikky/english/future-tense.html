Future Tense

📅 11th May 2026

📖 What is the Future Tense?

The future tense shows an action that will happen in the future — later, tomorrow, next week!

✨ The Formula
Subject + WILL + base verb
💬 Examples
🏃
I will play.
🍽️
You will eat.
😴
He / She / It will sleep.
💃
We will dance.
🏠
I will go home after school.
🌧️
It will rain tomorrow.
🎉
We will have a party next week.
