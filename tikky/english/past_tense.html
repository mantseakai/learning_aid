Past Tense Time Machine!

Learn about things that already happened! ✅

The past tense is a verb form that tells us about actions that already happened — in the past!

If something happens TODAY we say it in the present. If it happened YESTERDAY or before, we use the past tense! ⏰

TODAY (Now)
I walk 🚶
→
YESTERDAY (Past)
I walked 🚶
📋 Two Types of Past Tense Verbs
➕

Regular Verbs

These are easy! Just add -ed to the end of the verb.

verb + ED ✅
✨

Irregular Verbs

These are special! They change into a completely different word.

verb → new word!
