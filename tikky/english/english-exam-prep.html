🏃 Present Continuous — is/are + verb-ING
📖 The Three Rules for adding -ING
Rule 1 — Most verbs: just add -ing
play → playing   eat → eating   meet → meeting
Rule 2 — Short vowel + consonant: DOUBLE the last letter!
run → running   hop → hopping   sit → sitting   swim → swimming
👆 Short vowel = a, e, i, o, u said quickly (like the u in run)
Rule 3 — Verb ends in silent -e: drop the e, add -ing
ride → rid~~e~~ → riding   write → writ~~e~~ → writing   make → mak~~e~~ → making

❓ WH Questions — What · When · Where · Why · Who · How
📖 Which question word to use?
WHAT — asking about a thing 🔍
"What is that?" · "What is your name?"
WHEN — asking about time ⏰
"When is your birthday?" · "When do you sleep?"
WHERE — asking about a place 📍
"Where is Jack?" · "Where do you live?"
WHY — asking for a reason 🤔
"Why is he sad?" · "Why are you late?"
WHO — asking about a person 👤
"Who is your teacher?" · "Who is at the door?"
HOW — asking about way / feeling 😊
"How are you?" · "How do you spell it?"

📦 Countable & Uncountable Nouns
📖 What is the difference?
COUNTABLE nouns — you can COUNT them! 1, 2, 3…
1 apple 2 cats 3 books 4 cups
They have a plural (add -s or -es): apples, cats, books
UNCOUNTABLE nouns — you CANNOT count them!
water milk rice bread sugar coffee
No plural — we say "some water" NOT "two waters"
💡 Test yourself: Can I say "one ___, two ___"?
✅ one apple, two apples → countable
❌ one water, two waters → uncountable

🖼️ Descriptive Writing — Write About a Picture!
📖 How to write a great sentence about a picture!
Step 1: WHO is in the picture? → The boy The girl They
Step 2: What are they DOING? → is climbing are flying is crying
Step 3: Add WHERE or WHAT → the tree a kite in bed
✅ Good sentences:
🪁 The children are flying kites.
🌳 The boy is climbing the tree.
😭 The girl is crying.
😴 He is sleeping in bed.
