Weather & Seasons ⛅

Learn about the sky, the air, and Ghana's two seasons! 🌍

Weather tells us how the air and sky are at a time.

Examples of weather: sunny ☀️ · rainy 🌧️ · windy 💨 · cloudy ☁️

Ghana has two main seasons — the rainy (wet) season 🌧️ and the sunny (dry) season ☀️

🌍 Ghana's Two Seasons
🌧️

Rainy (Wet) Season

It rains a lot! The ground gets wet and rivers fill up. Plants grow well during this season.

☀️

Sunny (Dry) Season

The sun shines and it is very hot! There is little or no rain. It can be dusty and dry.
