🖼️ What Is an Exhibition?
🏛️

An exhibition is a place where people display things they have made or learned.

When you show your drawings and paintings to your family — that is an exhibition! 🎉

🎨 Things in an Exhibition

👆 Tap each one to find out more!

✏️

Drawings

Pictures made with a pencil or pen! You draw shapes and lines.

🖌️

Paintings

Pictures made with paint and a brush. They can be very colourful!

🧶

Handcrafts

Things you make with your hands — like weaving, folding or cutting!

📝

School Work

Work you have done at school — writing, sums, projects!

🏺

Models

3D objects you build — like clay pots, paper houses or sculptures!

📋 How to Arrange an Exhibition
1️⃣
Choose Items

Pick the things you want to show. Choose your best work! 🌟

2️⃣
Arrange Them Neatly

Put your things in a nice, tidy order so everyone can see them clearly. 🗂️

3️⃣
Label Them

Write a small name card for each item so people know what it is. 🏷️

4️⃣
Invite People to See

Ask your family and friends to come and look at your wonderful work! 🎉
