Our Amazing Senses! 🌟

We have five senses that help us learn about the world every day!

🔍 Meet the Five Senses!

👆 Tap a card to flip it and learn more!

👀

Sight

👁️

We see with our EYES! We can see colours, shapes and people we love! 🌈

👂

Hearing

🎵

We hear with our EARS! We can hear music, voices and sounds! 🎶

👃

Smell

🌸

We smell with our NOSE! We can smell yummy food and nice flowers! 🍕

👅

Taste

🍭

We taste with our TONGUE! We can taste sweet, sour and bitter things! 🍋

✋

Touch

🤲

We feel with our SKIN! We can feel hot, cold, soft and hard! 🧊🔥
