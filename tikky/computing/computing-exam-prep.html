🔵 Motion blocks (blue) — make the sprite MOVE

🟣 Looks blocks (purple) — make the sprite TALK or CHANGE
say Hello! — makes the sprite show a speech bubble
🎵 Sound blocks (pink/purple) — make the sprite play a SOUND

🟡 Control blocks (orange/yellow) — REPEAT or WAIT

wait 1 seconds — pauses for 1 second
💡 Bigger numbers = faster / more movement!

💻 Programming Ideas
💡 Key Programming Facts
What is Scratch used for?
Scratch is used to make programs, animations, and games!
It is NOT for cooking food or sleeping! 😄
How do you add a block?
You drag and drop a block from the blocks palette into the coding area.
What makes a sprite move faster?
Changing the numbers in the blocks!
Bigger number = faster/more movement   Smaller number = slower/less
What does the repeat block do?
It makes something happen again and again! It does NOT stop or delete.
What do we use in Scratch to give instructions?
We use blocks — NOT paint, NOT paper!
