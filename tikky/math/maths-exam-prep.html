➕➖ Topic 3 — Addition & Subtraction

Adding and taking away

🍎 + 🍎🍎🍎 = 🍎🍎🍎🍎 = 4
Tim has 3, John has 4 → 3 + 4 = 7 (NOT 9! Count carefully!)
💡 Column addition: always add the ONES first, then the TENS.
    91 + 16 → ones: 1+6=7 · tens: 9+1=10 → write 107
💡 Column subtraction: take away the ONES first, then the TENS.
    80 − 10 → ones: 0−0=0 · tens: 8−1=7 → write 70

🔢 Topic 4 — Number Patterns

Patterns from the test — count in 2s and 3s!

+2: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12…
+3: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18…
💡 Count in 2s: always add 2 more each time → 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12
💡 Count in 3s: always add 3 more each time → 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18
💡 After 12 in the 3s pattern: 12 + 3 = 15, then 15 + 3 = 18
