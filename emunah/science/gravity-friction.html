🌍 Section 1: Gravity

Gravity is a force that pulls objects towards the CENTRE of the Earth.

It acts in all directions — objects on top, on the sides, even at the bottom are all pulled toward the centre!

🍎 🌧️ 🏠 🌙 ↓ ↑ → ←
🌍
Everything is pulled toward the centre!
🍎 Gravity keeps our feet on the ground.
🌙 Gravity keeps the Moon orbiting the Earth.
🌧️ Gravity makes rain fall downward.
💡 Without gravity, we would float away!
🔥 Section 2: Friction

Friction is the force between two objects when they RUB against each other.

Friction SLOWS DOWN moving objects.

Friction pushes AGAINST movement.

🧊

Slippery Surface

Little friction
Objects slide easily
Examples: ice, wet floor, glass
🏔️

Grippy Surface

Lots of friction
Objects don't slide easily
Examples: rough road, sandpaper, carpet
⚠️ Note: Too much friction can BURN your skin!
