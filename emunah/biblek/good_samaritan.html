The Good Samaritan

Luke 10 : 30 – 37

A story about compassion, kindness and being a true neighbour

📖 The Story

Read through the story. Tap any part to highlight it!

🏙️
A Jewish man was travelling from Jerusalem to Jericho when he was attacked, beaten and robbed by thieves.
🤕
Unable to move, the injured man lay by the side of the road, helpless and alone.
🚶
A priest came by, saw the injured man, but chose to pass by without helping.
🚶
A Levite also came by, saw the man, and also passed by without helping.
🧡
A Samaritan came along and took pity on the injured man. He covered him with his cloak and put him on his donkey.
🏨
The Samaritan took him to an inn. He bought medicine, wine, oil and bandages and gave money to the innkeeper to look after him.
💛
The Good Samaritan's act of kindness made him a true neighbour to the injured man.

💛 The Moral of the Story

  • Show compassion — care about others even if you don't know them.
  • A true neighbour is anyone who shows kindness and helps someone in need.
  • Don't walk past someone who needs help — be like the Good Samaritan!
  • Small acts of kindness can make a big difference in someone's life.
