Group 2 — Les nombres de 17 à 19
Dix (10) + the ones digit!
🟣 17, 18 and 19 follow a simple rule: say dix (ten), then add a hyphen, then the ones number. Think of it as "ten-seven", "ten-eight", "ten-nine"!

Group 3 — Les nombres de 20 à 69
Tens + ones — with a special "et" for 1s!
🟢 Rule for the ones: When the ones digit is 1, use et un (e.g. vingt-et-un = 21). For all other digits, just add a hyphen (e.g. vingt-deux = 22, vingt-trois = 23…).

⚡ Group 4 — Les nombres de 70 à 100
The TRICKY zone — French does maths with numbers!
🟠 These weren't filled in your notebook — so pay extra attention!

French doesn't have new words for 70, 80 or 90. Instead it uses maths:
  • 70 = soixante-dix → literally "sixty-ten" (60 + 10)
  • 80 = quatre-vingts → literally "four-twenties" (4 × 20)
  • 90 = quatre-vingt-dix → "four-twenties-ten" (4×20 + 10)
