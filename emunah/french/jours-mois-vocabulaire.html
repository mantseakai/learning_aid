⏰ Section 1 — Le Vocabulaire du Temps

Learn these important time words in French. Study the French word, its English meaning, and the example sentence.

aujourd'hui
today
Aujourd'hui c'est lundi.Today is Monday.
hier
yesterday
Hier, j'ai lu un livre.Yesterday, I read a book.
demain
tomorrow
Demain, il y a école.Tomorrow, there is school.
avant-hier
the day before yesterday
Avant-hier, c'était samedi.The day before yesterday was Saturday.
premier / première
first (m / f)
C'est le premier jour.It is the first day.
dernier / dernière
last (m / f)
C'est la dernière semaine.It is the last week.
mois
month
Il y a douze mois dans l'année.There are twelve months in the year.
semaine
week
La semaine a sept jours.The week has seven days.
Je suis né(e) en [mois].  →  Je suis née en mai. "I was born in [month]." → "I was born in May."

📅 Section 2 — Les Jours de la Semaine

The seven days of the week in French. Notice they are all lowercase!

⚠️

Important Capitalisation Rule!

In French, days of the week and months of the year do NOT begin with a capital letter — EXCEPT at the very start of a sentence.

✗ Lundi est mon jour préféré.
Wrong — Lundi is NOT at the start? Actually it is here — but don't capitalise when mid-sentence!
✓ Mon jour préféré est lundi.
Correct — lundi is mid-sentence so it is lowercase.
✗ Je suis née en Mai.
Wrong — Mai should never be capitalised.
✓ Je suis née en mai.
Correct — mai is always lowercase.
