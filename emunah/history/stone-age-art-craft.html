💎 Stone Age Jewellery

Stone Age people didn't just make cave art — they also made beautiful jewellery to wear and to show others how important they were!

Types of jewellery
📿Necklaces
⌚Bracelets
📌Pins

People attached pins to their clothes.

What was it made from?
🦴Small bones
🦷Animal teeth
🐚Shells
These were carved into special shapes and threaded onto a piece of cord.
What was the cord made of?
The cord was twisted from: animal gut, hair, or plant fibres.
Why did they wear jewellery?
👑 Wearing jewellery showed that a PERSON WAS IMPORTANT.
