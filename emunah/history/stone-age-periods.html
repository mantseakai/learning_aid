The Three Periods

🟠
Palaeolithic Age
Old Stone Age — the very first period
PALAEO = OLD
  • ⬛This is the earliest stage of human history
  • 🏹People were hunters and gatherers — they hunted animals and collected wild food
  • 🪨They used simple, rough stone tools
  • 🦇People lived in caves for shelter
  • 🔥They discovered fire
  • 🎨They made early art — cave paintings on the walls of their caves

Tap to glow 🟠

⬛
Mesolithic Age
Middle Stone Age — the second period
MESO = MIDDLE
  • 🔪Tools became smaller and sharper than before
  • 🎣People started fishing
  • 🐄People began to keep animals

Tap to glow ⬛

🟢
Neolithic Age
New Stone Age — the third period
NEO = NEW
  • 🌾People started farming — growing their own food
  • 🏘️People lived in villages instead of caves
  • 🪃They used smooth, polished tools — better than rough stone!
