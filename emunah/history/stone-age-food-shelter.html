Food & Shelter in the Stone Age 🏹🏕️

📅 7th May 2026
Long, long before houses, supermarkets, or farms, Stone Age people had to work very hard every day to find food and stay safe. Let's discover how they did it! 🌍
Key Word

🚶 Stone Age People Were NOMADS

A nomad is someone who moves from place to place in search of food — they do not stay in one home forever.
🦌 They followed animals they wanted to hunt. Wherever the animals went, the people went too!
🍖 Food in the Stone Age

Stone Age people found food in two main ways — by hunting animals and by gathering plants. Tap each item to learn more!

🏹 Hunted Animals

🦌 Deer 🐟 Fish 🦌 Antelope

Stone Age hunters used spears, bows, and arrows made from stone and wood to catch animals.

🫐 Gathered Plants

🍎 Fruits 🌰 Nuts 🫐 Berries 🌿 Green Plants 🌱 Roots 🌾 Wild Grain

Gathering means picking food that grows wild in nature — no farms needed!

Stone Age people did not have fire at first — they ate their food raw! Later they discovered fire and started cooking. 🔥
🏕️ Shelter in the Stone Age
🛡️ Stone Age people needed shelter to protect themselves from the weather (rain, cold, heat) and from wild animals.

Types of Shelter — tap each one!

🦇
Caves

Natural shelters found in rock — strong protection from wind and rain.

🏠
Huts

Simple structures built from natural materials — like branches and animal skin.

⛺
Temporary Shelters

Quick shelters built for a short time — because nomads kept moving!

🔨 Materials Used for Shelter — tap each to see how it was used!
🪨 Stones Used to build walls and weigh down shelters
🪵 Sticks Used as frames to hold the shelter up
🌿 Branches Used as roofing and walls to block wind
🐾 Animal Skin Used as a covering to keep warmth inside
🍃 Leaves Used on roofs to keep rain out
🌾 Grass Woven together to make thick, warm walls
